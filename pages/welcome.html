Welcome to ClearHead

When you open an explicit site, ClearHead gently brings you to a short breathing exercise, so you can let the urge pass instead of acting on it.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no servers, no tracking. Nothing you do here ever leaves your computer.

It only ever sees the sites it blocks. ClearHead can read the sites on your blocklist, and nothing else you browse.

You're already protected. A built-in list of common sites is on right now. You can add your own any time, and Chrome will ask permission for just that one site.

There's no bypass button, by design. Removing a blocked site takes a calm five-minute cooldown.
