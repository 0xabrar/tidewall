Welcome to ClearHead

When you open a porn site, ClearHead gently redirects you to a short breathing exercise — so you can ride the urge out instead of acting on it.

  • Everything stays on your device No account, no servers, no tracking. Nothing you do here ever leaves your computer.
  • It only ever sees the sites it blocks ClearHead can read the sites on your blocklist — and nothing else you browse.
  • You're already protected A built-in list of common sites is on right now. Add your own any time — Chrome will ask permission for just that one site.

There's no bypass button — by design. Removing a blocked site takes a calm 5-minute cooldown.
