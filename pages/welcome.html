Welcome to ClearHead

When you open an explicit site, ClearHead brings you to a short breathing exercise instead, so the urge can pass.

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

It only ever sees the sites it blocks, nothing else you browse.

A built-in list is already on. You can add your own any time.

Using Incognito?

Chrome keeps extensions off in Incognito until you allow it. To stay protected there, open chrome://extensions, click Details on ClearHead, and turn on Allow in Incognito.
